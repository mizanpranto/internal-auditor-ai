# Northwind Analytics Ltd - Access Control Procedure (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

Version 1.4 - Owner: Head of IT

## 1. Principles

Access to systems and data is granted on a need-to-know and least-privilege basis. Access is never shared between people, and every user has a unique account.

## 2. Joiners, movers and leavers

New access is requested by the line manager through the IT service desk and approved by the system owner before it is granted. When an employee changes role, their previous access is reviewed and removed if no longer needed. When an employee leaves, HR notifies IT on the last working day and all accounts are disabled within 24 hours.

## 3. Access reviews

System owners review the list of users and permissions for all business-critical systems every quarter. Any access that is no longer justified is removed and the review is recorded in the service desk.

## 4. Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access, for email, and for every administrator account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Accounts are locked after five failed sign-in attempts. Passwords are stored in the company password manager and must never be written down or shared.

## 5. Privileged access

Administrators use a separate named admin account that is distinct from their day-to-day account. Admin accounts are limited to the IT team and are reviewed every quarter together with the general access review. Shared administrator accounts are not permitted.
