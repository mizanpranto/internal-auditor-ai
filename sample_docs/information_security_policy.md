# Northwind Analytics Ltd - Information Security Policy (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

Version 2.1 - Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

## 1. Purpose and approval

This policy sets out how Northwind Analytics Ltd protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information. The policy is approved by the Board of Directors, published on the company intranet and communicated to all employees and contractors on joining. The policy is reviewed at least once a year or after any significant change, and each review is recorded in the version history.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

The CISO is responsible for the information security management system. An ISMS Steering Committee made up of the CISO, the Head of IT, the Head of HR and the Data Protection Officer meets quarterly. Every department head is accountable for security within their team. All employees must follow this policy and report suspected security events.

## 3. Acceptable use and classification

Company systems and information may be used only for authorised business purposes. Information is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Confidential and Restricted information must not be stored on personal devices or sent to personal email accounts. Each classification level has handling rules published in the Data Handling Standard.

## 4. Assets

IT maintains a register of laptops, servers and software licences. Each department keeps its own list of business-critical data sets.

## 5. People security

All employees sign a confidentiality agreement as part of their employment contract. Security awareness training is mandatory for every employee at onboarding and then once a year. Completion is tracked by HR and reported to the Steering Committee.

## 6. Remote working

Employees working remotely must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Company laptops use full-disk encryption. Public Wi-Fi may be used only together with the VPN.

## 7. Suppliers and legal requirements

Suppliers that process Confidential or Restricted data must complete a security questionnaire before a contract is signed. The company complies with the GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018. Legal reviews data protection obligations when new services are launched.
