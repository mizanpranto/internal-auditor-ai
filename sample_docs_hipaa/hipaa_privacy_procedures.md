# Riverbend Family Clinic - Privacy Procedures (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

Version 2.4 - Owner: Privacy Official

## 1. Privacy Official

The Clinic Director has designated the Practice Manager as the Privacy Official. The Privacy Official is also the contact person who receives privacy complaints and questions. Contact details are printed in the Notice of Privacy Practices.

## 2. Notice of Privacy Practices

A Notice of Privacy Practices is given to every new patient at the first visit, and patients are asked to sign an acknowledgement. The Notice is displayed in the waiting room and published on the Clinic website.

## 3. Uses and disclosures

Staff use and disclose patient information only for treatment, payment and health care operations unless the patient has signed a written authorization. Authorization forms include all required elements and the patient may revoke an authorization in writing. Staff are trained to disclose only the minimum necessary information for requests that are not for treatment, and each role has a defined level of access to the record.

## 4. Patient rights

Requests for access to health records are answered within 30 days. Patients may ask for a copy in electronic form, and the fee is limited to the cost of copying. Requests to amend a record are reviewed by the treating clinician and answered within 60 days. The Clinic keeps a log of disclosures that must be included in an accounting of disclosures.

## 5. Training

All workforce members complete privacy and security training within 30 days of joining and every year after that. Training completion is tracked by Human Resources, and managers are notified of overdue training.

## 6. Complaints and non-retaliation

Patients may file a complaint with the Privacy Official in person, by email or by post. Each complaint and its outcome is recorded in the complaints log. The Clinic does not retaliate against anyone who files a complaint or exercises a privacy right.

## 7. Safeguards for paper and verbal information

Paper records are kept in locked cabinets. Printed documents that contain patient information are shredded. Staff must not discuss patient information in public areas.
