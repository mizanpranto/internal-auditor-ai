# Riverbend Family Clinic - Information Security Policy (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

Version 3.0 - Owner: Security Official

## 1. Purpose and roles

This policy protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of electronic protected health information (ePHI) held by Riverbend Family Clinic. The Clinic Director has appointed the IT Manager as the Security Official, who is responsible for developing and implementing these policies and procedures. Every employee, contractor and volunteer who can reach ePHI is part of the workforce and must follow this policy.

## 2. Risk analysis and risk management

A security risk analysis covering every system that creates, receives, stores or transmits ePHI is carried out each year in January and after any major system change. The analysis lists threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood and impact for each system. Risks rated High are added to the risk register, and the Security Official reports the register to the Clinic Director each quarter. Treatment dates for Medium and Low risks are not currently tracked.

## 3. Workforce security

New staff receive access to ePHI only after their manager submits an access request and the Security Official approves it. Background checks are completed before an offer of employment is confirmed. When a person leaves, the Security Official disables their accounts on the last working day using the offboarding checklist. Staff who violate this policy are subject to sanctions up to and including termination, and each sanction is recorded by Human Resources.

## 4. Access control and authentication

Every user has a unique user name. Shared accounts are not permitted. Access to the electronic health record (EHR) is role based, and access reviews are carried out twice a year by department leads. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access and for administrator accounts. Workstations lock automatically after ten minutes of inactivity.

## 5. Audit logging and review

The EHR records every view, change and export of a patient record. Logs are retained for six years. The Security Official reviews access reports for unusual activity every month and records the review in the log review register.

## 6. Encryption

All laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption. Backups are encrypted. Encryption of the EHR database at rest is planned for next year. Email containing ePHI must be sent through the secure messaging portal, and connections to the EHR use TLS.

## 7. Physical safeguards

The server room is locked, and entry is limited to the Security Official and IT staff. Visitors sign in at reception and are escorted. Workstations in patient areas face away from public view. Hard drives are wiped using an approved tool and a certificate of destruction is kept when equipment is retired.

## 8. Business associates

Before a vendor is given access to ePHI, the Clinic signs a business associate agreement with the vendor. The Security Official keeps a list of business associates. The list has not been reviewed against actual vendor usage in the last two years.

## 9. Documentation

Policies and procedures are reviewed every year. Previous versions are kept for six years from the date they were last in effect, and the current version is available to all staff on the intranet.
